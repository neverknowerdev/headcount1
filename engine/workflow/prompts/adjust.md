Phase: adjustment.

Something did not go to plan: work failed, a review kept asking for changes, verification did not pass, or the task was handed back to you. Goal: work out why, and decide what to do differently.

Look at what failed and at the decisions that led there; read reports in full or the decision tree if the summaries are not enough. Then act: delegate new or corrected work with `create_tasks`, try failed tasks again differently with `retry_tasks`, go to verification with `finish_adjustment` if the results are sufficient as they stand, or `escalate` if no plan can meet the definition of done. Do not repeat an approach already recorded as a dead end.
